# Noise-bias samples

Stratified samples at different physical bit-flip rates. They test reweighting across noise bias and show when a flag setting ceases to meet the target.

[Summary table](../summary/transfer_check.md) gives the derived comparison. Each JSON stores its `spec`; [the matching task file](../../tasks/flag_bias.jsonl) reruns this folder. [Methods](../../docs/methods.md) explains the estimator and assumptions, and [reproduction](../../REPRODUCE.md) covers fresh samples and saved-output checks.
